# Content Policy

This repository is **public** and **non-commercial**. Everything committed here is visible to
anyone, and Git history preserves what was published even after it is deleted.

> **This is unofficial fan content, created and shared for non-commercial use. It has not been
> reviewed by Dragonsteel Entertainment, LLC or Brotherwise Games, LLC.**

That notice is required by the [Cosmere RPG Fan Content Policy](https://www.cosmererpg.com/fan-content-policy),
which is what this repository operates under. It appears on the site and in the README, and it
must stay there.

## Game rules and tables

**Rules, tables and mechanics needed to play may be used here.** A table that a player needs in
order to make a character belongs in the hands of that player. Reproducing a discovery table in
a dice tool, quoting a rule so a house rule makes sense, restating a procedure in our own words —
all of this is what a table does, and Brotherwise's policy encourages exactly this kind of
non-commercial fan work.

Cite the source when it helps — book and page — so the reader knows where the rule comes from and
that the book is worth owning. This repository is a convenience for a table that has the books,
not a substitute for them.

## What stays out

The limits that remain are not about rules. They are about other people's property, other
people's money, and this table's secrets.

**Never publishable, no exceptions:**

- Books, commercial PDFs, EPUBs or scans, whole or in part. Using a table is not the same as
  redistributing the book it came from.
- Artwork, covers, logos, maps and illustrations belonging to Dragonsteel, Brotherwise or any
  third party.
- Trade dress: page backgrounds, border elements and cover templates from official products.
  Brotherwise's policy names these specifically.
- Anything monetised or paywalled. This work stays free, and that is a condition of the fan
  policy, not a preference.
- Any personal data of players: legal name, email, phone number, address.

**Not publishable without a decision:**

- GM notes, campaign secrets, clocks, fronts, future scenes, faction agendas and the true
  identity of antagonists.
- Internal mechanics, prototypes and studies not yet approved.
- Anything past the current spoiler line (see `SPOILER_POLICY.md`).
- Heavy working files: PSD, AI, INDD, video, raw exports.
- Google Drive shortcuts (`.gdoc`, `.gsheet`, `.gslides`) and `.lnk`, which preserve no content
  in Git.

## The practical test

If it helps a player play, it can be here. If it spoils a discovery still ahead, redistributes
someone's book or art, or makes money, it cannot.

## Before publishing

1. Does this help the table, or does it spoil something still in play?
2. Is any of it art, logo, trade dress, or a chunk of a book rather than a rule in use?
3. Does anything cross the current spoiler line?
4. Do images have authorship, process and licence on record in `docs/assets/ATTRIBUTION.md`?
5. Is the fan content notice still present on the site and in the README?

## If something wrong gets published

Deleting the file **is not enough** — the history remains. Remove the content, tell Mario, and
decide explicitly between rewriting history (`git filter-repo`) and recreating the repository.
Do not improvise.
