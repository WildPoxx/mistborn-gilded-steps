# Spoiler Policy

## The current line

Public continuity in this repository runs **through the end of the second novel of the Second
Era**. The campaign opens in **Bilming, 343 PC**, after those events.

Nothing past that line may be published here — not as a mention, not as a proper name, not as
a piece of trivia — without an explicit, recorded decision from Mario.

## How to handle what falls outside

Events in Elendel and the rest of Scadrial reach Bilming as **news, rumour, a shift in the
market, or distant political pressure**. That is how they appear in public material: by the
shadow they cast over the city, never as an account of what actually happened.

Characters from the novels **do not appear** in this campaign. Do not write them as present,
active or reachable.

## The Set

The Set is a systemic, compartmentalised force in the background. Public material may record
that the city is changing under pressures nobody can fully name. It may not record the
organisation's structure, its real objectives, its members, or its connection to canon events.

## Three layers

| Layer | Where it lives | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | this repository | Yomend Steps is a dockside district under redevelopment pressure. |
| Table | revealed in play, then published | who bought the warehouses on the east quay. |
| GM | the vault only | why they bought them, and on whose orders. |

A fact moves up a layer by **decision**, never by habit. When a secret is revealed at the table
and Mario approves, it can be rewritten for the wiki — rewritten, not copied from the GM note.

## Revision

This line is revisited when the campaign has moved far enough to warrant it. The revision is
recorded in the project's decision register in the vault before anything changes here.
