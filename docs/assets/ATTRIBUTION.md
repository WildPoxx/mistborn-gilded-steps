## Two origins, two rules

**Art brought by the players.** Character portraits and tokens are produced by the players
themselves and granted to the project. The choice of tool is theirs. What this repository needs
is only the record of origin, authorship and authorisation — the line in the table above. There
is no tooling requirement.

**Art commissioned by the project.** Illustration commissioned by the project for setting,
handouts or pages follows the visual identity directive in the private vault: a defined
channel, a brief written first, and the prompt recorded alongside the asset.

## Rules that apply to both

- No asset, from any source, is presented as official art of *Mistborn*, Brotherwise,
  Dragonsteel or Brandon Sanderson.
- No covers, maps, logos or illustrations belonging to third parties, resized, cropped or
  filtered though they may be.
- Images of real people require recorded authorisation.
- Prefer `.webp`, or optimised `.jpg`/`.png`. Above 500 KB, justify it.
