Metalborn Path

Second Era · character creation

Roll to discover whether your character is Metalborn, and which power they woke to. The tray walks the whole branch: the path first, then the metal, and both if you come out Twinborn. It never chooses for you where the rules give you a choice.

The roll gives you a choice. On this result the rules let you take either path. Pick the one you want and the tray will roll its metal.
