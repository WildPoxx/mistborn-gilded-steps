Gilded Steps

The city climbs in steps. Below, the quays, the warehouses, the rail yards and the houses held on old rents. Above, the clubs, the offices, and the windows that look down at the harbour the way a man looks at an inventory. This is the way in for players — and it keeps no secret that has not yet been found at the table.

The city

Bilming prospers and does not share. New cranes, new rail, new façades — and on the same block, contracts that shorten, rents that climb, and trades that vanish without notice. Industrialisation did not unseat the aristocracy: it bought shares.

Yomend Steps, called simply The Steps by the people who live there, is the low strip of the waterfront: dockhands, railway workers, workshops, boarding houses, gambling rooms, and families who have been there for generations. Above the slope, the high steps watch. What is decided up there arrives down here as an eviction, as construction, as an offer that cannot be refused.

This campaign is not about saving the world. It is about what you do when the world has an owner, and the price of staying where you are climbs every month.
