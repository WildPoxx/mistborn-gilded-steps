Metallic Arts

In the novels, Allomantic and Feruchemical powers are inherited, and so they fall unevenly across the population. If you want to discover your character's powers alongside them rather than choosing, roll on these tables. The three below are the ones from the Mistborn Handbook, laid out as they appear there, each with its own roll.

Randomizing Your Metalborn Path

Roll first

Roll on this table, then take the path in the column for the era you are playing in. If you land on Misting, Ferring or Twinborn, go on to the table for that art below.

House rule, First Era. The GM of that table widened Misting and made the full sets rare: 5–18 Misting, 19 Mistborn, 20 Feruchemist. The Handbook's own spread is 5–14 Misting, 15–17 Mistborn, 18–20 Feruchemist — a 15% chance of each full set, against 5% here. Nothing not woken stays at 1–4. The Second Era column is the book's, unchanged.

First Era. There are no Ferrings and no Twinborn — those come after the Catacendre. A high roll here gives the whole thing: 15–17 is a true Mistborn, every Allomantic metal, and 18–20 a full Feruchemist.

Second Era. The Ferrings appeared and the full sets did not survive. The high rolls now give halves: a choice between two paths, or Twinborn — one power from each art.

Randomizing Your Misting Power

Allomancy

Roll here for your Allomantic power. If the metal is not available in your era, reroll — the roller does that for you and shows you both rolls.

First Era. Chromium, nicrosil, cadmium and bendalloy were not discovered yet — they are struck out below and the roller rolls past them. Aluminum, duralumin, gold and electrum are the Steel Ministry's secret: the book calls it very unlikely that a character knew of them before the Lord Ruler died. The God Metals of this era — atium, malatium, lerasium — are not on this table; they are not rolled for.
