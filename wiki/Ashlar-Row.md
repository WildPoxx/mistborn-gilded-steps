# Ashlar Row

> **Original to this campaign.** Ashlar Row is an old residential row in Knightbridge, Bilming.

Narrow, tall houses stand in line above stone steps. At the back, yards, washing lines, service
doors and small favours bind the street more tightly than any city plan. It is the kind of
place where a key stays at the bakery, where shopping climbs one extra flight, and where a pot
travels from house to house.

## A street in change

Knightbridge is being redeveloped. Around the row still standing, new housing appears — bright,
standardised, sold as the future of the district. For some people that means comfort or
opportunity; for others it means hard contracts, high rents, and the sense that their own
address no longer fits their life.

The **Bright Potential Society** is a familiar name in conversations about the Knightbridge
Redevelopment. As in any city growing fast, there are promises, suspicions, and people trying
to work out who pays for the change.

## Places to meet people

- **The corner bakery:** the smell of the oven, a short queue, neighbourhood credit, and
  conversations that stop when a stranger walks in.
- **The steps:** at the end of the day the houses turn towards the street; news, bets, requests
  for help and small arguments all find an audience.
- **The backs:** washing lines, gates and service passages connect more than the maps suggest.
  Respect the privacy of the people who live there.
- **The new strip:** shops, newly built houses, and workers who are also just trying to make a
  life work.

## What matters to a newcomer

Ashlar Row is not a ready-made cause, nor a community with a single voice. Some want to stay;
some want to leave safely; most just want to get through the month without someone else
deciding their future.

Characters build ties by offering work, listening to a story, paying for a meal, helping with a
repair, or simply learning not to treat the street as scenery. Trust is earned — and can also
be lost.

## Where it sits on the campaign map

Ashlar Row is in **Knightbridge**. Yomend Steps, or **The Steps**, lies closer to the
waterfront, the quays, the warehouses and the rail. The two places are connected by people,
cargo, work and rumour, but they are not the same district.
