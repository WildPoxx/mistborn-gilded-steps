# Bilming

> **In preparation.** This page takes on setting material as it is cleared for players.

A port city on the **Yomend Sea**, and the stage of the campaign in **343 PC**.

## What is already public

Bilming is a harbour city in accelerating industrialisation: docks, rail, tenements, canals,
factories, private salons and mansions rising on the slope. The prosperity is real and the
distribution is not.

Modernisation did not replace the old order. The aristocracy persists and re-articulates itself
around industrial capital, around the administration, and — where convenient — around crime.
Wealth here works to look inevitable.

What happens in the rest of Scadrial — in Elendel above all — reaches Bilming as news, rumour,
a shift in the market, or distant political pressure. Not as a visit.

## Districts with their own page

- [Yomend Steps](Yomend-Steps.md) — the low strip of the waterfront, at the port.
- [Ashlar Row](Ashlar-Row.md) — an old residential row in Knightbridge, under redevelopment.

The two sit at different points of the city and are connected by people, cargo, work and
rumour — not by being neighbours.
