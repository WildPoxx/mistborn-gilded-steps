# Chronicle

The record of the campaign, session by session — written for players, with no backstage and no
reveal of what has not yet been discovered.

> **Nothing published yet.** The campaign has not begun.

## How these work

Each entry is published **after** the session and reviewed first. It tells what the characters
lived through and what became known. It does not tell what was behind it, nor what is still in
play.

A fact enters here only after it has been revealed at the table. The chronicle does not, by
itself, alter campaign canon: it records what happened.
