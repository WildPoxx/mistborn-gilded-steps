# Glossary

> **In preparation.** Terms are added as they come up at the table.

Terms from Scadrial, the Second Era and this campaign that players run into during play. Short
definitions, no spoilers; the depth belongs on the setting pages.

## From this campaign

| Term | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Yomend Steps** | The dockside district of Bilming, on the waterfront. Original to this project. |
| **The Steps** | What the people who live in Yomend Steps call it. |
| **Ashlar Row** | An old residential row in Knightbridge, under redevelopment. Original to this project. |
| **Mendari** | A community of Terris descent living in the district. Original to this project. |
| **Metalborn** | Anyone who has woken an Allomantic or Feruchemical power. See [Metallic Arts](Metallic-Arts.md). |
| **Twinborn** | Someone with one Allomantic power and one Feruchemical power. |

## From the setting

| Term | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **PC** | *Post-Catacendre*. The Second Era's count of years. The campaign opens in 343 PC. |
| **Bilming** | The port city on the Yomend Sea where the campaign takes place. |
| **Yomend Sea** | The sea Bilming stands beside. |
| **Knightbridge** | The Bilming district where Ashlar Row stands. |

Official rules and setting terms are looked up in the Cosmere RPG sources. This site does not
reproduce their definitions.
