# Metalborn Lottery

**House rule.** Original to this campaign, built on top of the official Second Era table for
discovering the Metallic Arts. Approved for use; still pending playtest validation.

This page **does not reproduce** the official table. Consult it in the *Mistborn Handbook*,
p. 129 (`Metallic Art Discovery`, Era 2) — the GM has the book at the table.

## 1. First discovery

Roll `1d20` on the official Era 2 `Metallic Art Discovery` table.

**Every result except a natural `1` ends the lottery.** Build the character down the official
path for whatever came up.

## 2. Natural 1: the Second Chance

If the first die shows exactly `1`, you choose:

- **take the official result** (`Non-Metalborn`); or
- **take the Second Chance** and roll the `d20` below once.

That second roll can never be repeated.

| d20 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 19-20 | `Mistborn` — an exceptional anomaly; talk to the GM before finishing the sheet. |
| 15-18 | `Twinborn` — use the official path. |
| 9-14 | `Non-Metalborn` with a `Single Burden`. |
| 3-8 | `Non-Metalborn` with a `Double Burden`. |
| 1-2 | `Lucky Anomaly` — an exceptional result, defined individually with the GM. |

A `Mistborn` or a `Lucky Anomaly` is not licence to ignore limits on creation, training,
metals, goals or crew balance. They are rare results that call for a conversation at character
creation.

## 3. Burdens

A `Burden` is the mechanical consequence of having taken the Second Chance. It is written on
the sheet and applies **always** to the `Skill Tests` indicated — it does not depend on the GM
remembering it in a scene.

It does **not** reduce `Attributes`, `Defenses`, `Health`, `Focus` or `Investiture`.

### Domains

| Domain | Attributes of the affected Skills |
| --- | --- |
| `Physical Domain` | `Strength` and `Speed` |
| `Cognitive Domain` | `Intellect` and `Willpower` |
| `Spiritual Domain` | `Awareness` and `Presence` |

### Single Burden

Choose **one** Domain. Whenever you make a `Skill Test` for a Skill tied to it, apply
1 `Disadvantage`.

You choose, **before rolling**, which eligible die takes the `Disadvantage` — normally the
`d20` or the `plot die`.

### Double Burden

Choose one of two options:

1. **`Split Burden`** — choose two Domains. You take 1 `Disadvantage` on `Skill Tests` tied to
   either of them.
2. **`Deep Burden`** — choose one Domain. You take 2 `Disadvantages` on `Skill Tests` tied to
   it. If you roll both `d20` and `plot die`, both are affected.

`Advantages` and `Disadvantages` cancel under the game's usual rules.

## 4. The Burden in the character's story

A Burden **need not represent a disability**. It can reflect a health pressure, a debt, social
stigma, scrutiny of one's bloodline, a family obligation, or any other difficulty you want to
explore.

Characters with disabilities do **not** receive a Burden automatically. If you want a
disability to have mechanical effects, talk to the GM about specific limitations and
accommodations. An accommodated character works normally, in line with the *Handbook*.

The point of the rule is to create choices, stories and real risk — never to treat a body, a
mind or a social origin as a moral failing.

## Quick summary

```text
Roll the official table (Handbook, p. 129).
Natural 1? Choose: keep Non-Metalborn, or take the Second Chance.
Single Burden = 1 Domain, 1 Disadvantage.
Double Burden = 2 Domains with 1 Disadvantage each, OR 1 Domain with 2 Disadvantages.
```
