# Metallic Arts

How your character discovers whether they are Metalborn, and which power woke in them.

**This table uses the official method** from the *Mistborn Handbook*: roll on the Metallic Art
Discovery table, then on the table for the art you landed on. Nothing is house-ruled.

## Roll it here

The three tables live on the portal, laid out as they appear in the book, each with a roll
underneath and the row you landed on lit up:

**[→ Metallic Arts tables](https://wildpoxx.github.io/mistborn-gilded-steps/metallic-arts.html)**

You can also roll real dice and read off the same tables. The page is a convenience, not an
authority — the book is.

## How it goes

1. **Roll `1d20` on Metallic Art Discovery.** Read the Second Era column; this campaign opens in
   343 PC.
2. **`1–4` — None.** No Metallic Art woke in you. This is the most common outcome in the
   population, and it makes a character, not a shortfall.
3. **`5–9` — Ferring.** Roll on Feruchemy Discovery for your metal.
4. **`10–14` — Misting.** Roll on Allomancy Discovery for your metal.
5. **`15–17` — Ferring *or* Misting, your choice.** Pick the path you want, then roll its table.
   This one is a decision, not a die.
6. **`18–20` — Twinborn.** Roll on both tables: one Allomantic power and one Feruchemical.
   Twinborn combinations have names of their own — ask the GM about yours.

A `Mistborn` or any other exceptional outcome is not licence to ignore the limits on creation,
training, metals, goals or crew balance. It is a rare result that calls for a conversation at
character creation.

## Retired: the Metalborn Lottery

An earlier house rule for this table — the **Metalborn Lottery**, with a second chance on a
natural `1` and permanent Burdens by Domain — is **no longer in use**. The table now uses the
official method above. The house rule stays on record in the GM's vault as a design study; it
does not apply in play.
