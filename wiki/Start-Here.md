# Start Here

## What this campaign is

A **Cosmere RPG** campaign set in **Bilming, Scadrial, 343 PC**, in the Second Era of
*Mistborn*. Urban fantasy of uneven modernisation: industry, rail, the port, gunfighters,
organised crime, and an aristocracy that has re-articulated itself around capital.

## What it is not

- Not epic. Nobody is going to save the world.
- Not a closed mystery. Investigation is a tool for understanding networks and acting on them,
  not for naming the culprit in the last chapter.
- Not resource management. Money and logistics enter play when they illuminate class,
  dependency, territory or ambition — not as a spreadsheet.
- Not a tour of the novels' canon. It is a parallel story in the same city.

## What it is

The tone is **grey**. Choices cost something, social position matters, and no institution or
group is purely virtuous — the characters' allies included.

Three priorities of play, in this order:

1. **Social relations** — alliances, contests of influence, the games of power.
2. **Urban exploration** — territory, institutions, doors that open and close.
3. **Crime and consequence** — rising, dealing, conflict, and the price of all of it.

Action and violence exist as a possible consequence of conflict, not as the objective.

## What a character should be

Someone who **belongs to the city**: with an address, a trade or the lack of one, people they
owe favours to and people who owe them. Concrete ties in Yomend Steps are worth more than a
mysterious past.

The characters occupy a borderline position, close to a rising gang. That makes them neither
villains nor heroes. It makes them people with interests.

Each character's file lives in the [Constabulary Record](https://wildpoxx.github.io/mistborn-gilded-steps/constabulary-record.html) —
the archive the Bilming Constabulary keeps on you.

## Before the first session

1. Read this page and the [Home](Home.md).
2. Read the [Metalborn Lottery](Metalborn-Lottery.md) — it changes how your character finds out
   whether they have the Metallic Arts.
3. Read the [Constabulary Record](https://wildpoxx.github.io/mistborn-gilded-steps/constabulary-record.html) and send the GM your
   identification details.
4. Read the [Table Rules](Table-Rules.md).
5. Bring two things: someone your character depends on, and someone who depends on them.

## About spoilers

The current line is **the end of the second novel of the Second Era**. If you have read
further, leave it out of play. If you have read none of them, that is fine — the campaign was
designed to work without that reading.
