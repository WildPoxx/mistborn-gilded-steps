# Table Rules

> **In preparation.** The full table contract will be published before session zero.

## What already applies

### House rules in force

- [Metalborn Lottery](Metalborn-Lottery.md) — replaces the standard discovery of the Metallic
  Arts. Approved; pending playtest validation.

No other house rule is in force. Proposals under study do not apply at the table until they are
announced here.

### Agency

Adventures are situations of pressure and choice, not required paths. There is no "right
answer" the GM is waiting for. There is what you decide to do, and what follows from it.

### Consequence

Choices cost something. Social position matters. No group, institution or ally is purely
virtuous — and that includes the characters' allies.

### Spoilers

Current line: **the end of the second novel of the Second Era**. Anyone who has read further
keeps out of play what the characters would have no way of knowing. Full policy in
[SPOILER_POLICY](../SPOILER_POLICY.md).
