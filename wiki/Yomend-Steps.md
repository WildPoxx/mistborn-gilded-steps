# Yomend Steps

> **In preparation.** The district fills in as the table discovers it.

**Original to this campaign.** A district on Bilming's waterfront, named for the Yomend Sea.
The people who live there call it **The Steps**.

## The shape of the place

A low strip of quays, warehouses, rail yards, small industry and workers' housing, pressed
against a slope. Above it, on the high steps, old houses, clubs, offices and the properties of
wealthy families command the view of the harbour.

High and low are not only topography. They are two cities sharing an address.

## Who lives there

Dockhands, canal and coastal sailors, railway workers, workshop hands, families on old rents,
small traders, and people displaced from other districts.

The advance of harbour, industrial and luxury residential development threatens contracts,
homes and local ways of working. That is why The Steps is the campaign's microcosm: capital,
aristocracy, crime, labour and survival all meet on the same ground.

## The night seam

The district has a night quarter of ambiguous reputation: bars, boarding houses, gambling
rooms, music, sex work, and the meeting points of workers, smugglers and informants.

It is not merely a scene of degradation. It is real social infrastructure — income, mutual
protection, exploitation, memory and danger, all on the same block. Its name, its houses and
its codes will be published once the table establishes them.
